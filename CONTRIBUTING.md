# Contributing

We welcome bug reports and suggestions as issues. **We accept code, documentation or data contributions only from people who have signed our Contributor Licence Agreement (CLA).** The CLA lets Franklin Softworks LLC license your contribution under GPL-3.0-only and under other terms, including commercial terms. You keep your copyright. To sign it, email legal@franklinsoftworks.com before you contribute. We cannot accept contributions under a Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off alone.

Patches to LibreOffice files keep the licence of the file they modify.

If any part of your contribution was generated by an AI tool, say which part when you submit it.
